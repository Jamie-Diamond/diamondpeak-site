Wetsuit Predictor v2.0

Will it be a wetsuit or non-wetsuit swim at IRONMAN Italy Emilia-Romagna? This tool uses seven independent prediction methods — empirical, model-based, and observational — to triangulate the likely water temperature on race day and estimate the probability of wetsuit-legal conditions.

Changelog: 16 Jul 2026 (data review) – Historical data re-verified against the Open-Meteo marine archive: the August mean SST for 2023 (27.8°C), 2024 (29.8°C) and 2025 (27.4°C) all still match source to the decimal, and no race-day reading changed. The API-failure fallback for August SST was refreshed from 26.2°C to 28.3°C (the 2023–25 archive mean) so a dropped live fetch no longer defaults cold – this only affects predictions when the live marine API is unavailable. For context, the current summer is running hot (~29–30°C in mid-July, well above the 2023–25 baseline); the far-out prediction reads this live via Method 7.
v2.0 – Split the model into two explicit stages, so a sea-temperature forecast is never confused with what the referee's thermometer reads at 6am. Stage 1 predicts sea-surface SST; Stage 2 walks that to the official reading through a measured diurnal step (−0.26 ± 0.10°C, the 6am-vs-daily-max gap over 90 September days of hourly archive) and a measured coastal-reading step (median −0.40°C from n=3 race days, ±1.7°C). Both steps are now shown in a table on the page. Four bugs fixed: (1) the CLI never fetched the SST forecast beyond 9 days out; (2) the engine discarded the whole forecast series unless it reached within 2 days of race day – it is now bridged to race day at the measured cooling rate; (3) the measurement bias was derived from seatemperature.info but applied to Open-Meteo inputs, two sources that differ by 1.7°C on 2023 race day – the offset is now measured against the same instrument the live feed uses; (4) Method 1 is a mean of official readings and was still being bias-corrected a second time. Method weights now decay with lead time: the year-blind historical mean and climatological normal held a fixed 37% of the weight in v1, twelve days out, with the sea 4°C above normal. The live-anomaly decay (5%/day) is gone – the archive shows the warm anomaly grew through September in 2023 and 2025 and collapsed only in 2024, so the anomaly now persists in the mean and collapse risk sits in a fat cold tail. That cold-event risk applies at every lead time, not just the last five days. The NE-wind detector was recalibrated after archive checking showed it never fired – not even in 2024, whose collapse came from NW wind at 300–311°, and whose peak September wind never reached the old 30 km/h floor. Cold-tail variance now combines in quadrature rather than being added linearly, and cold-wind days already past are no longer charged twice against a live observation.
v1.3 – Method 7: current-summer anomaly with seasonal persistence (e-folding ~65 days) – the far-out forecast now reacts to how hot the sea is running TODAY vs the 2023–25 same-window baseline, instead of ignoring pre-August observations. Data revision from a source sweep of all Cervia editions (full + 70.3): 2022 corrected to an athlete-reported 21°C wetsuit swim (the race was storm-delayed a day; the old 25.0 was pre-storm satellite – the same wind cold-drop as 2024, whose 21.5 reading the satellite archive confirms against media reports of 23). 2023 satellite value aligned with its cited source. No official 70.3 readings survive online; a 2019 athlete report (21–22°C) conflicts with the previous day's official 24.6 and is excluded pending resolution.
v1.2 – Method 6: physical ocean-model forecast SST (highest weight in race week). Leave-one-out backtest panel; ensemble uncertainty floored at the measured historical error (MAE ~2.2°C weeks out – the probability is now honest about it). Bora/NE-wind cold-tail adjustment via split-normal (a late Bora is how 2024 went mandatory-wetsuit). Per-year August SST from the marine archive (2023–25).
v1.1 – Defaults now target the next upcoming race (2026: Saturday 19 September). Added the 2025 race to the history (satellite SST 25.2°C; official reading not found, flagged as estimated). Estimated years excluded from the measurement-bias calculation. Fixed a variable-ordering bug in the August SST fallback. Prediction maths extracted to a shared engine (js/wetsuit-engine.js) used identically by this page and the ClaudeCoach bot.
