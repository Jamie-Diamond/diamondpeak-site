Your health comes first

  • Check with a doctor before you start if you have a heart condition, any other medical condition, an injury, or are pregnant, or if you are unsure whether you are fit to train.
  • Stop and get medical help if you have chest pain, faintness, unusual breathlessness or sharp pain while training.
