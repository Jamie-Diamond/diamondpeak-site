Privacy

How Peak, the Diamond Peak coaching app at coach.diamondpeak.uk, uses your data. In short: we use it to coach you, we never sell it, and you can take it with you or have it deleted at any time.

Last updated 15 October 2026

How Peak uses AI

Peak's coach is an AI model: Claude, made by Anthropic. It reads your training, your wellness data and what you tell it, so it can write your plan, review your sessions and reply to your messages.

  • It is used only to coach you.
  • It only works on your data with your explicit consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing us, and your coaching then stops.
  • AI can get things wrong. If something in your plan doesn't feel right, tell the coach or email us.
  • Peak's coaching is not medical advice. If you are ill or injured, see a medical professional.

Who we are

Peak is run by Diamond Peak Consulting in the UK. We are the data controller for the data Peak holds about you. Questions or requests: jamie@diamondpeak.uk.

What we collect

DataWhere it comes from

Much of this is health data, which UK law treats as special. We only use it with your explicit consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

What we use it for

  • To write and adjust your training plan
  • To send you coaching messages, session reviews and food feedback
  • To put planned workouts on your Intervals.icu calendar so they reach your watch
  • To keep the service running and fix problems

We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you use Peak. If you leave Peak or ask us to delete your data, we delete it within 30 days, including from backups.

We may keep anonymised statistics that combine many athletes and cannot identify you, to improve Peak's coaching.

Your rights

  • See or export everything we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your account and data
  • Withdraw consent or disconnect Intervals.icu or Strava at any time

Email jamie@diamondpeak.uk to do any of these. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in Peak before it takes effect.
